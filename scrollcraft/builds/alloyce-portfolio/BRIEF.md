References are an engineer's annotated working papers and a project review, not a cinematic landscape.

3. **Energy.** Authored: a composed opening, a quiet project introduction, one precise transformation, then a calmer explanation and resolved invitation. No continuous spectacle.
4. **Feeling and remembered moment.** Authored: curiosity becomes trust when the visitor can trace the same field from its source through a check and review boundary. The record remains identifiable throughout.
5. **Signature.** Authored: “Follow one record.” A persistent record plate and four accessible inspection steps reveal the actual seeded PeopleOS email exception, the real validation rule, the proposed source-of-truth value, and the audit boundary. This is explicitly a local illustration. It does not impersonate a live session or claim an approval occurred.
6. **Range.** Existing user-approved larger type and warm paper/forest identity stay. FinPulse retains its separately established cobalt identity. Authored range is an editorial engineering desk; no glossy AI gradients, invented metrics, or photo assets unrelated to the work.
7. **World.** Authored distinct scenes in normal document flow. No continuous world, scrolljacking, forced video playback, or long pinned argument.
8. **Assets.** Real DOM, self-hosted DM Sans and Instrument Serif, genuine PeopleOS and FinPulse captures, and recorded product videos. No generation, API key, paid imagery, or stock portrait is required.

## Grammar and fingerprint gate

**Project desk** is a custom grammar with actual structural constraints: an ordinary object index in the top navigation; real application captures already on the first screen; natural-flow project dossiers; one field inspection between project contexts; an explicit project choice at the end that carries into working links. Copy, photographs of screens, and operable demonstrations are labeled separately.

**Bans:** fixed marketing bar, full-bleed footage under claims, scrolljacking, sequential full-screen pins, magnetic buttons, numeric section counters, animated statistics, fake terminals, and repeating reveal animations on every card.

The eight supplied grammars were considered: filmic one-shot and continuous world obscure reviewable project evidence; live surface is appropriate to the apps themselves but would turn this personal portfolio into a third fake product; chaptered editorial excludes the required opening media; typographic poster underuses genuine product assets; gallery treats all evidence as equally weighted objects; split stage invents a conflict between complementary projects; rhythmic cutlist conflicts with the requested readability. This grammar is a review desk, not a renamed filmic page.
